First release. A calm, precise chromatic tuner, with presets for guitar, bass down to low B, ukulele, violin, viola, cello, mandolin and banjo, plus your own tunings, microtonal if you like. Tap a string to hear it plucked and tune by ear. Singers and fretless players get a practice view: your pitch drawn on a piano roll, with a tanpura drone to sing against. Adjustable A4, light and dark themes, and nothing is ever recorded.
