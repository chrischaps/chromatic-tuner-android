More tunings: guitar half-step down, DADGAD, open G and open D; bass 4- and 5-string down to low B; low-G and baritone ukulele; violin, viola, cello, mandolin and banjo. Make your own tunings, up to eight strings, and fine-tune any string in cents for microtonal tunings. Half-step down reads in flats. New: tap any string to hear it plucked and tune by ear. A ringing low E no longer jumps to D3.
